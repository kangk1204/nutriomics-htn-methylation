# Supplementary reciprocal transfer protocol

This protocol is declared after observing the frozen primary nested CV and forward GSE193795→GSE42774 results. It does not replace them and does not create an untouched external validation cohort. Its purpose is to examine direction sensitivity when one public cohort trains a classifier and the other cohort supplies evaluation outcomes.

Source is GSE42774: hypertension8/control8, eight published matched participant pairs. Target is the previously evaluated GSE193795 hypertension44/control44. preHT44 stays outside fitting, tuning and binary evaluation. Source and target have distinct deposited GSM IDs; this alone is not comprehensive participant de-duplication across publications. Population, platform, preprocessing and demographic differences remain. Individual age/sex adjustment is unsupported by the available metadata.

The common 10,749 measurement IDs and processed matrix bytes must match the original primary frozen hashes. Per-measurement detection-P QC was fixed during preparation. Every learned missingness filter, median imputation, variance ranking and standardization fits source training samples only. No target cohort normalization, batch correction, target-selected features, threshold fitting, prediction inversion or coefficient sign flipping is permitted.

Both ridge and elastic net are retained. The grid matches the primary analysis: k100/500, C0.01/0.1/1, elastic-net l1 ratios0.1/0.5, training probe missingness≤0.10, decision threshold0.5. Source OOF uses four outer group folds and three inner group folds; final source16 fitting uses four inner group folds. Every published matched pair stays in one fold. Selection ranks mean source inner-fold AUROC with stable listed-grid ties. The target88 outcomes are used only to score completed predictions.

Report source pair-CV OOF and target transfer AUROC, average precision, Brier, sensitivity and specificity for both families. Discrimination/Brier intervals use 1,000 bootstraps of realized predictions, source matched-pair resampling and target stratified participant resampling. They do not capture repeated fitting/model-selection uncertainty. Sensitivity/specificity intervals are exact binomial intervals. Tiny source size can yield unstable preprocessing and hyperparameter choices; apparent increases do not establish clinical improvement.

Run `nutriomics-methylation transfer --root REPO --cores 12`. New artifacts are isolated in `results_transfer/42774_to193795` and `docs/transfer`. Existing `results` and `docs/validation` artifacts remain frozen. Independently collected, more comparable, previously unseen cohorts are preferable for future generalization evaluation. An additional new dataset requires a separately recorded source/outcome protocol and must not be added under this protocol silently.
