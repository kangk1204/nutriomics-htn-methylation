**Figure. Source-selected CpG signature and cross-tissue twin transfer.**

**a**, All 50 probes from the frozen obesity model, ordered by source coefficient rank. Cells show mean methylation differences in beta percentage points: obese minus lean in discovery and EPIC adipocytes, and higher minus lower BMI within 24 discordant monozygotic twin pairs in bulk subcutaneous adipose tissue (GSE68336). Grey crosses retain four unavailable EPIC measurements. Gene labels are native Illumina hg19 annotation associations; 11 unassigned loci remain explicit. Diamonds mark paired twin BH q < 0.05 within the 50 source-selected probes. Discovery effects are descriptive following feature selection.

**b**, Independently replayed discrimination. Source nested CV is a tuned source pipeline; it is not fixed-50-probe nested CV. Already-seen EPIC and GSE67024 cohorts were predicted with the unchanged frozen model. Twin transfer uses the same model without target fitting, tuning or calibration. Bars are conditional 95% bootstrap intervals; GSE68336 resamples twin families. These intervals do not quantify full model-selection uncertainty. GSE68336 measures a within-family BMI ranking in a different tissue; it is not an absolute clinical-obesity validation.

**c**, Four twin loci passing BH correction across the 50 preselected probes. Estimates and two-sided paired-t 95% confidence intervals use 24 families (23 degrees of freedom). TMEM100 and CDH13 are native gene-body annotations; cg08125291 and cg05378452 remain unassigned. Methylation change does not establish altered expression or a causal gene mechanism.

국문 보고서용: 기존 비만 판별 모델과 예측값의 독립 재실행을 통해 nested AUROC 0.932, EPIC 재현 AUROC 0.915, GSE67024 재현 AUROC 0.981을 확인하였다. 동일 모델을 BMI가 서로 다른 일란성 쌍둥이 24쌍의 피하 지방조직에 적용한 결과 AUROC는 0.700(가족 단위 bootstrap 95% CI 0.583–0.818)이었다. 50개 CpG 중 48개에서 원 자료와 동일한 메틸화 차이 방향을 보였으며, 선택된 50개 위치 내 다중검정 보정 후 4개가 유의하였다. TMEM100·CDH13과의 연결은 유전자 본체 위치에 대한 주석이며 발현 변화나 인과관계를 의미하지 않는다. 전체 지방조직과 분리 지방세포의 차이, 임상적 비만과 쌍둥이 내 상대 BMI의 차이를 고려한 탐색적 전이 결과로 기술한다.
